Toilet and cistern repair services

A running cistern or water that will not stop

A toilet that continues running after a flush needs the cistern components and relevant connections assessed. We identify the part of the fixture involved and whether compatible replacement parts are suitable. Tell us whether water is running into the bowl, leaking outside the toilet or affecting the surrounding floor.

Slow filling, weak flushing and controls that fail

Filling and flushing problems are not all the same repair. We assess the toilet type, controls, accessible supply connection and reported behaviour before setting the scope. A concealed cistern may need a different access plan from an exposed one; any access work is discussed before it is assumed in the quote.

Blocked toilets and overflow

When waste will not clear or the bowl rises during use, we assess whether the problem is at the fixture or in the drainage serving it. Other affected drains are useful information for the visit. If several fixtures back up, our blocked-drain clearing service covers the wider drainage scope.

How we assess and complete a toilet repair

Our plumber checks the reported fault, the fixture condition and the components that can be safely accessed. We explain whether the proposed work concerns the flush mechanism, filling components, connections or drainage. That distinction helps avoid approving a fixture replacement when the actual request is a component repair or a blocked drain.

Component repair or a replacement toilet

The condition of the toilet, compatible parts and access influence the options. A replacement toilet needs its installation requirements assessed separately; connection positions and fixture dimensions matter. We discuss the parts or fixture to be supplied and any additional connection work before you approve the written quote.

For the agreed work, we check the relevant filling, flushing or repaired connection and explain the outcome. If water around the toilet comes from an unclear source, we discuss a separate leak investigation rather than treating every wet floor as a cistern fault. Rental jobs also need the approval contact and arrangements for access to the bathroom.

Our Perth plumbing and electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed trades. Licence details are available on request.

Safety: Avoid contact with wastewater and keep people away from an overflowing toilet. Do not continue using an affected fixture if it is backing up. For immediate danger, Call 000.

Blocked Toilet & Toilet Repairs Perth FAQs

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

What if the toilet is running constantly?

Tell us whether it is continuously running, whether there is water on the floor and whether the fixture can still be used.

What should I do if it is overflowing?

Avoid further use and keep people away from contaminated water. Contact us with the property details and the situation.

Could a blocked toilet involve other drains?

Tell us if another fixture is also slow, gurgling or backing up. That context helps distinguish a toilet-specific concern from a broader drainage issue.

Can you help with multiple toilets at one property?

Yes. Identify each affected fixture and its symptoms in the enquiry.

Does every running toilet need to be replaced?

No. We assess the component fault, condition and availability of compatible parts. A complete replacement is a separate option with its own installation requirements and written scope.
