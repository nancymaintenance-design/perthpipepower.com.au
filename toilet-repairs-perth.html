Toilet Repairs Perth

A direct repair path for running, leaking, slow-filling or non-functioning toilets in Perth homes and managed properties.

Call 0413 477 667
What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • A toilet keeps running after flushing
  • Water appears at the base or around the cistern
  • The cistern fills slowly or does not refill normally
  • The toilet is blocked, backs up or cannot be used
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

Toilet problems should be described by what the fixture is doing, not by a guessed part name. Tell us whether the toilet is usable, whether water is reaching the floor, whether it is continuously running and when the behaviour started.

Ellis Services can assist with toilet repair and maintenance enquiries across Perth. For a blocked toilet with overflow risk, avoid continued use and make this clear when you contact us.

Frequently asked questions

Toilet Repairs Perth FAQs

What if the toilet is running constantly?

Tell us whether it is continuously running, whether there is water on the floor and whether the fixture can still be used.

What should I do if it is overflowing?

Avoid further use and keep people away from contaminated water. Contact us with the property details and the situation.

Can you help with multiple toilets at one property?

Yes. Identify each affected fixture and its symptoms in the enquiry.
