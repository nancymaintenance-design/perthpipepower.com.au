Blocked-drain clearing for kitchens, bathrooms and toilets

Slow sinks, showers and floor drains

Drainage that slows repeatedly needs an assessment of where the restriction affects the system. We distinguish a problem at a single fixture from a blockage involving several drainage points. Tell us which sink, shower or floor drain is affected, how long it has been slow and whether using another fixture changes the symptoms.

Blocked toilets and wastewater backing up

A blocked toilet, water rising in a bowl and wastewater returning through a drain require different attention from a cistern that will not fill or stop running. We assess the drainage involved and explain the proposed clearing work. For a flush mechanism or leaking cistern rather than drainage, see our toilet repair service.

Recurring blockage rather than a one-off restriction

If a drain blocks again after being cleared, let us know what previous work was completed. Repeat symptoms can require further investigation rather than repeating the same treatment without checking the wider drainage. We discuss the access points and findings before recommending any separate repair work; the enquiry alone does not establish the cause.

How we scope drain clearing and further repairs

We begin with the affected fixtures, the extent of the backup and the available drainage access. Our plumber assesses the appropriate treatment for the identified restriction and explains what the clearing visit covers. Any additional investigation, access work or pipe repair is identified separately so a simple clearing request does not become an undefined job.

Clearing the obstruction versus repairing the drain

Clearing addresses the restriction affecting flow. Repair is a separate scope where the assessment identifies a damaged section or another issue needing corrective work. We explain that distinction and the evidence from the visit before asking you to approve additional work. We do not promise that every recurring blockage can be resolved by one identical method.

After the agreed work, we check drainage at the relevant points and explain any remaining limitation or recommended follow-up. For rental and managed-property repairs, include the access contact and approval pathway. Our Perth plumbing services cover related fixture and water-supply repairs alongside drainage.

Blocked Drains Perth — Drain Clearing & Repairs FAQs

Blocked drainage: clearing or fixture repair?

A running cistern is a fixture problem; wastewater rising in a bowl or returning through another outlet is a drainage symptom. Recurrence after clearing may need investigation, but it does not by itself prove roots, a collapsed pipe or a need for replacement.

  • Running cistern and flush repairs
  • Water outside the drainage system
Does a recurring blockage mean the drain must be replaced?

Not automatically. The condition and cause need assessment. Ask what the initial clearing covers and what evidence would justify further investigation or repair before approving excavation or replacement.

Is a camera inspection included in every drain-clearing job?

Do not assume it is included. Ask whether further investigation is needed, what method is proposed and whether it is a separate scope and charge. Availability of a particular inspection method should be confirmed for the job.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

What details help with a blocked-drain request?

Tell us the affected fixture, whether water backs up elsewhere, when it began and whether the issue is getting worse.

Should I keep using a blocked fixture?

Avoid using a fixture that is overflowing or causing water to return elsewhere. Do not use chemicals as a substitute for a repair assessment.

Can a blocked toilet be related to a drain issue?

It can be useful to note whether other fixtures are slow, gurgling or backing up. Use the Toilet Repairs page for a toilet-specific concern and include all visible symptoms in the enquiry.

Can you help with property-managed drainage issues?

Yes. Include the property reference, access details and approval contact.

Does a blocked-drain quote include pipe replacement?

Clearing and pipe replacement are different scopes. We identify the proposed treatment and any separate investigation or repair in the written quote before additional work proceeds.
