Plumbing and electrical repairs in Perth Hills & Swan Valley

For Perth Hills and Swan Valley properties, include the suburb and any driveway, gate, site-contact or access detail that is relevant to the repair visit. Keep electrical, hot-water, drainage and leak symptoms separate.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For a hot-water problem, report whether the change is in supply, temperature, pressure or visible water around the system. For a drain or leak concern, describe the affected fixture or visible location without opening fittings or walls.

If access to the system, meter area or outdoor fixture needs a gate, key or site contact, include that information with the repair request so the visit can be planned appropriately.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For power faults, lighting or safety-switch concerns, describe the affected area and the safe observations you can make. Do not test fixed wiring, remove covers or repeatedly reset a switch to collect more information.

Where electrical equipment is wet, damaged, smoking or sparking, keep clear. Call 000 for immediate danger rather than approaching the equipment.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

For homes, rentals and managed properties, add the address or suburb, access instructions, service type and a concise symptom description. Property managers can include the work-order reference and approval contact.

A clear repair request separates plumbing and electrical items so the Perth team can consider the correct scope for each concern.

Clear scope before work

Written quote before work proceeds

The Perth team uses the repair category, reported symptom, site access and any assessment required to define the work. A written quote confirms scope and price before work proceeds.

If additional work is identified during assessment, it is explained before it is added. Keep the completed-work and invoice information with the property records.
