Leak detection and plumbing repairs for Perth properties

Visible pipe and connection leaks

Water around a connection, fixture or accessible pipe gives us a useful starting point but does not always show the full path of the leak. We assess the affected plumbing and surrounding area before setting the repair scope. Tell us whether the leak is constant, occurs during use or has changed since you first noticed it.

Suspected leaks behind finishes or in concealed plumbing

Dampness, recurring moisture and unexplained water around a property need a source investigation before repair access is agreed. We distinguish what is visible from what still needs checking. Opening walls, flooring or another finished surface is not assumed as part of every detection job; the reason and proposed access are discussed with you.

Leaks at bathroom and kitchen fixtures

A leak can involve a tap, flexible connection, supply pipe or drainage connection. Identifying the relevant component helps separate a fixture repair from concealed pipework. For a leak clearly centred on the fitting, our tap and mixer repair service explains that scope; an active damaged pipe belongs with burst pipe repairs.

From locating the source to agreeing the leak repair

Our plumber reviews the symptoms, accessible plumbing and the pattern of water use associated with the leak. We explain the investigation needed to narrow down the source. The work required to find a leak and the work required to repair it can be different tasks, particularly when the affected pipe is not exposed.

Access, repair and reinstatement are separate decisions

Once the source and access requirements are established, we explain the affected section or connection, the proposed repair and any additional work. The quote identifies the agreed plumbing scope and whether access or reinstatement is included. Do not assume that replacement finishes, painting or other building work are part of the plumbing repair unless stated.

Following the approved work, we check the repaired connection or section and explain the outcome of the visit. We discuss any further moisture investigation or follow-up identified during the assessment, so the plumbing repair and any separate building work have a clear next step. Any additional work is explained and quoted before you approve it.

Our Perth plumbing and electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed trades. Licence details are available on request.

Safety: Keep away from water near electrical fittings or wiring. Do not open a wall or remove equipment covers to investigate. For immediate danger, Call 000.

Water Leak Detection Perth FAQs

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Can you help with a hidden water leak?

Yes. Describe the property area, any visible moisture and changes you have noticed. This helps us understand the request before work is arranged.

What should I include in a leak enquiry?

Include the address, affected room or outdoor area, when the issue began, whether water is active and any safe photos.

What if water is near electrical equipment?

Keep clear of the area and do not touch wet electrical equipment. If there is immediate danger, move to safety and call 000.

Are locating the leak and repairing it the same job?

They can be separate parts of the scope. We explain the investigation, access and proposed repair before work proceeds, and identify any additional access or reinstatement work in the written quote.
