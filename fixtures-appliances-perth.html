Fixture, Tap & Appliance Connection Services in Perth FAQs

Do I need detailed installation specifications before contacting you?

No. Tell us the fixture, equipment or upgrade you need and your Perth suburb. We assess the site and connections, confirm the requirements and qualified work arrangements and provide the written quote. Additional access is agreed with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

What information helps with a fixture or appliance connection enquiry?

Include the fixture or appliance, the room, the issue you have noticed, the property address and whether water, drainage or electrical supply is involved.

Can plumbing and electrical work be considered together?

Yes. Explain the complete requirement so the relevant plumbing and electrical work can be considered as part of the scope.

How is the work priced?

Pricing is based on the agreed scope, property access, materials or parts required and the condition confirmed through the enquiry or assessment process.

Does appliance connection include appliance servicing?

Connection work and repair of the appliance itself are different services. We identify the installation and connection tasks in the written quote and explain the boundary if the request involves an internal appliance fault.
