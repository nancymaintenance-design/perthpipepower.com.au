Lighting, switches and power-point services

Existing lighting and switch faults

For a light that does not operate correctly, we assess the fitting, controls and relevant installation before defining the repair. Tell us which lights are affected and whether the issue is constant or intermittent. A fault at one fitting is different from several lights losing power together; wider interruptions may require electrical fault finding.

Power-point repairs and replacement

Loose, damaged or unreliable outlets need a licensed electrical assessment. We establish the condition of the existing installation and the proposed replacement requirements rather than asking you to remove the outlet. Tell us the room, intended use and observable issue so the scope addresses the actual connection or fitting involved.

New lights, relocated outlets and renovation work

An extra power point or a new lighting position needs more than selecting the visible fitting. We assess existing wiring, access, intended use and the installation requirements. For planned work, send fitting specifications and preferred positions; cable routes and any additional circuit work are confirmed before the installation is quoted.

How we plan lighting repairs and electrical installations

We review the affected fittings or proposed locations and assess the relevant installation. Repairing an existing fault, replacing a fitting and adding a new point are different jobs. Our electrician explains the required connection, cable or circuit work so the quote matches the work to be completed rather than only naming a product.

Fitting compatibility and the work behind the finish

Customer-supplied lights and controls need to be suitable for the existing installation. Access above ceilings or behind finishes can affect the work, and any additional access is discussed in the scope. We confirm whether equipment supply, installation and related wiring are included; neither compatibility nor a simple swap is assumed before assessment.

After the approved work, we check the relevant installation and explain the completed scope. If the planned work includes linked controls or automation, our smart-home electrical services cover those integration requirements. We discuss the operation of the installed fittings and the next steps for any separately agreed upgrade.

Our Perth plumbing and electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed trades. Licence details are available on request.

Safety: Do not use a damaged, wet or overheating outlet and do not remove fittings, covers or wiring to investigate. Keep clear of a hazard. For immediate danger, Call 000.

Lighting & Power Point Repairs and Installation Perth FAQs

State whether the job is a repair or a new installation

For flickering LED downlights, identify which lights are affected without removing fittings. For a new power point or light, describe the intended position and use. Access, existing circuits and product compatibility determine the installation scope. A failed light does not prove that the bulb alone needs changing.

  • Fault finding beyond a single fitting
  • Lighting controls and compatibility
Can a new power point use the existing circuit?

That needs assessment of the installation, intended use and circuit requirements. Do not assume that every proposed location can use the nearest outlet or that no additional cable or circuit work will be needed.

Should I buy replacement LED fittings before the visit?

Discuss the proposed product and existing controls first. Fitting, driver and dimmer compatibility can affect the work. Confirm who supplies the product and what installation is included in the quote.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Can you repair a loose or damaged power point?

Yes. Stop using it and describe the location and visible condition in your enquiry.

What if a light only flickers sometimes?

Tell us which fitting is affected and when the behaviour occurs. Do not dismantle it to investigate.

Is a warm outlet urgent?

Stop using the outlet and report the warning sign. If there is smoke, fire or immediate danger, move clear and call 000.

Can you quote for a new outlet as well as a repair?

Yes. We distinguish repairing the existing installation from adding or relocating an outlet. The written scope identifies fittings, cable routes, access and any additional circuit work required for the proposed installation.
