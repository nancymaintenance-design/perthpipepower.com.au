Signs to report when you contact us

Start with what is happening.

  • One or more lights stop working or flicker
  • A switch, outlet or fitting is loose, cracked or discoloured
  • An outlet feels unusually warm or has a burning smell
  • Power changes when another device is used
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

Describe the fitting and its location rather than trying to inspect the internal wiring. Useful information includes whether one or several items are affected, whether the fault is intermittent and whether there are visible warning signs such as damage, heat, buzzing or a smell.

Our dedicated Perth electrical team provides lighting and power-point repairs for Perth residential, managed and commercial properties. Stop using anything that is damaged, sparking, buzzing, unusually warm or smells burnt.

Site-specific scope

What affects the work and written quote?

The fitting or outlet type, existing wiring, access and intended use affect the work. Repairing an existing connection is a different scope from relocating an outlet or adding new lighting. We establish the installation requirements on site and include fittings, cable or circuit work in the written scope where required.

Clear observations support the right next step.

Flickering, a damaged fitting, heat, an outlet that does not operate or a change in normal use are useful observations. Do not remove covers.

A symptom can have more than one cause. We arrange an on-site assessment, check the reported symptoms and confirm the repair plan and written quote.

If there is immediate danger, Call 000. Do not put yourself at risk to take a photo or investigate the cause.

Lighting & power points questions

Good to know before you enquire.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Lighting & Power Points Perth FAQs

Can you repair a loose or damaged power point?

Yes. Stop using it and describe the location and visible condition in your enquiry.

What if a light only flickers sometimes?

Tell us which fitting is affected and when the behaviour occurs. Do not dismantle it to investigate.

Is a warm outlet urgent?

Stop using the outlet and report the warning sign. If there is smoke, fire or immediate danger, move clear and call 000.
