  • Confirm the practical work sequence, including any coordination with other approved equipment providers.
  • Agree the electrical scope before installation, alteration or upgrade work proceeds.
A clear electrical assessment

Useful details before a visit.

Provide the equipment make and model where it is safely readable, what you want the system to do, and any symptoms or changes you have noticed. Photos of accessible labels or controls can be useful when they can be taken safely.

Please do not remove covers, reset protective devices repeatedly, or dismantle electrical equipment to investigate. Clear information lets the electrical work be considered without creating an additional safety risk.

Pricing and scope

How smart-home and energy work is scoped and quoted

Electrical upgrade work is quoted against the agreed outcome, existing accessible installation, equipment requirements, access and the materials or circuit work required. The scope is confirmed before installation or alteration work is arranged.

When a property needs investigation before the final work can be identified, an assessment may be the appropriate first step. Any change to the agreed scope is discussed before additional work proceeds.

Safety first

Leave electrical equipment intact.

Please do not remove covers, test wiring or dismantle equipment before contacting us. Include model details and photos only where they can be taken safely.

Frequently asked questions

Smart Home & Energy Electrical Services Perth FAQs

What should I include in a smart-home electrical enquiry?

Include the property address, the equipment or control you are considering, the rooms affected and the outcome you want to achieve.

Can existing energy equipment be assessed?

Describe the equipment and the behaviour you have noticed. Leave covers and electrical connections in place until the work can be assessed safely.

How is smart-home or energy electrical work priced?

Pricing is based on the agreed electrical scope, equipment requirements, property access, materials and any circuit work confirmed through the enquiry or assessment process.
