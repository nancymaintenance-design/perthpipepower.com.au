Managed properties

A clear maintenance handover between tenants and property managers

Maintenance requests can lose important detail when they pass from an occupant to a property manager and then to a service contact. A short structured handover keeps observable facts together.

Use this checklist

  1. Property address and unit number
  2. Occupant’s description in their own words
  3. Affected room, fixture or circuit
  4. When it began and whether it is worsening
  5. Safe action already taken
  6. Photos taken safely
  7. Availability, access, pets, parking or induction details
  8. Work-order reference and approval pathway
  9. Shared service or strata information

Keep updates in the same email thread or work-order record where practical. If a slow drip becomes an active flow, state the new condition and time clearly.

Keep the original occupant report separate from the repair instruction

A tenant may report a wet cupboard, a toilet that keeps running or lights that have stopped working. Preserve those observations in the handover. Do not rewrite them as an established broken pipe or failed circuit before assessment. The original symptom, affected room and timing help the service team investigate the correct item.

The manager then adds the property reference, authorised contact and access arrangements. This creates a traceable path from the occupant report to the approved work without asking the occupant to decide the technical repair or approve costs on behalf of the owner.

Update the condition when it changes

If a stable drip becomes continuing flow, or one affected fixture becomes several, send an update with the time and current condition. An appointment based on the first report should not continue to describe a minor symptom when the situation has materially changed.

Report hazards promptly using the property maintenance process. Do not delay contact while collecting a complete set of photos or documents. Keep clear of electrical equipment in contact with water; for immediate danger, move to safety and call 000.

Separate handover, access and work approval

This handover guide concerns passing the report between people. Use our repair-visit access checklist for practical entry arrangements and our plumbing and electrical work-order guide for the tasks and approval scope.

When the request is ready for assessment, our Perth property management maintenance service coordinates the nominated contact, reported issues and agreed quote. Keep the work completed and any further recommendation with the same property record so the next request does not rely on an incomplete handover.

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos.
