A clearer way to send a property maintenance enquiry

Useful maintenance information keeps the property address, observable issue, current status, access arrangements and approval contact together. It reduces the chance that an important detail is separated from the request.

Start with facts

Describe what is happening rather than assuming a cause. Include when it began, whether it is changing, and whether there is an immediate safety concern. Property managers can add the work-order reference and nominated decision-maker.

Choose a service starting point without diagnosing the fault

You can request service from an observed problem: a backed-up sink, visible water, loss of hot water or lights that have stopped working. Start with the affected area and what changed. You do not need to know which part has failed or which repair method will be used.

Choose blocked drain clearing for drainage backing up, water leak detection for unexplained moisture or leaking pipework, or electrical fault finding for reported power faults. If the issue spans plumbing and electrical equipment, describe both observations so the relevant trades can be considered.

State the current condition and property location

Include the suburb or address, affected room and when the issue started. Say whether water is still flowing, whether a fault is recurring or whether the service has stopped completely. These details help define the assessment request without requiring an occupant to test equipment.

For a planned installation, explain what you want connected or changed and where it will be used. Do not describe an appliance connection or new lighting location as a fault if it is an improvement request. This helps distinguish investigation from installation when the work is quoted.

From first contact to an agreed visit and quote

Our local team reviews the reported work, confirms the relevant access contact and arranges the on-site assessment. The findings determine the proposed repair or installation scope. You receive a written quote for the agreed work, with additional requirements explained before they proceed.
