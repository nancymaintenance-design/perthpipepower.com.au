What access details help with a CBD apartment visit?

Include the unit and building reference, nominated on-site contact and any agreed concierge, lift or parking arrangements. Keep private access codes out of public messages and confirm how they will be shared with the team.

What should a CBD or Inner Perth repair request include?

Include the property address, building or unit details, access requirements, observed issue and whether the repair is plumbing or electrical.

Can a property manager request plumbing and electrical repairs together?

Yes. List each issue separately, then include the access contact and approval pathway for the property.
