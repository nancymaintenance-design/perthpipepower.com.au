Plumber and electrician in CBD & Inner Perth

Plumbing and electrical repairs in CBD & Inner Perth

For an apartment, strata or managed property, include the building name, unit or level, access contact and any lift, concierge, key or parking instructions that affect the repair visit.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For a kitchen, bathroom or laundry plumbing concern, describe the affected fixture and what changed: slow drainage, gurgling, overflow, visible water, a dripping tap or loss of hot water. This lets the repair request start with facts rather than a guessed cause.

If the problem affects more than one fixture, list each symptom separately. For active water or an overflow, avoid dismantling fittings or attempting to contain water where doing so could put you at risk.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For a power fault in an apartment or inner-city property, identify the affected room, fitting or circuit only from a safe position. Report a safety switch that will not reset, lighting that has stopped working, a loose outlet or a power loss in a particular area.

Do not open a switchboard, remove covers or test fixed wiring. If smoke, active arcing or electrical equipment in contact with water creates immediate danger, move clear and call 000.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

Property managers can include the work-order reference, occupant contact, access window and approval contact with the repair request. Keeping property access and repair details together helps avoid unnecessary follow-up before scope is confirmed.

Where plumbing and electrical issues are both reported, list them as separate items. This helps the Perth team assess each repair requirement without assuming the two issues have the same cause.

Clear scope before work

Written quote before work proceeds

Our Perth team uses the reported symptom, property details and access information to identify the likely repair scope. The written quote confirms the work and price before work proceeds.

If assessment identifies an additional issue, it is explained before it is added to the repair scope. Keep completed-work and invoice information with the property records for future reference.

CBD & Inner Perth plumber and electrician FAQ

What should a CBD or Inner Perth repair request include?

Include the property address, building or unit details, access requirements, observed issue and whether the repair is plumbing or electrical.

Yes. List each issue separately, then include the access contact and approval pathway for the property.
