Hot Water Problems Perth

Help for no hot water, reduced temperature, inconsistent supply, pressure changes or visible water near a hot-water unit.

Call 0413 477 667
What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • No hot water at any fixture
  • Hot water that is cooler, intermittent or runs out sooner
  • Visible water near the unit or its connections
  • A change in water pressure or unusual noise from the unit area
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

Hot-water problems can involve plumbing, electrical components or both. The safest useful information is the unit location, what has changed, which fixtures are affected and any readable model details visible without removing a cover. Do not open a hot-water unit or handle any wiring.

Ellis Services Group can assess and coordinate hot-water repair and maintenance enquiries in Perth. If water is near electrical components, do not touch the unit. Keep clear and let us know what you can observe safely.

Frequently asked questions

Hot Water Problems Perth FAQs

Is a hot-water problem plumbing or electrical?

It can be either, depending on the system and symptom. Describe the issue rather than trying to diagnose it yourself.

What should I send with a hot-water enquiry?

Include the property address, unit location, what changed, affected fixtures and any safely readable unit information.

Can I remove the cover to check the unit?

No. Do not remove covers or handle wiring.
