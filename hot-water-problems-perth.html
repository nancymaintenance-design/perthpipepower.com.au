Hot-water repair services for Perth homes and managed properties

No hot water or supply that runs out early

A complete loss of hot water and a system that heats but cannot keep up are different service requests. We establish which outlets are affected, whether the change is constant or intermittent, and the type of equipment serving the property. This helps focus the assessment on the unit and supply rather than replacing parts on the basis of one symptom.

Temperature, pressure and leaking connections

We assess inconsistent temperature, reduced hot-water flow and visible leaks around accessible connections. A problem at one shower can have a different scope from a problem affecting every hot tap. The repair plan distinguishes the hot-water equipment from local fixtures, pipework and connections so you can see what work is actually needed.

Plumbing and electrical work through one Perth team

Where a hot-water fault involves electrical components as well as water connections, we coordinate the relevant licensed trades within the agreed job. Tell us the system location and any readable model information; you do not need to identify the failed component. See our hot-water plumbing and electrical fault guide for the distinction.

How we assess and complete your hot-water repair

Our visit starts with the reported loss of service, the equipment location and safe access to the installation. We inspect the relevant connections and investigate the reported fault. Before repair work, we explain the findings, the proposed parts or connection work, and any access or additional trade requirements that affect the scope.

Repair or replace the hot-water system?

Repair is assessed against the condition of the existing installation, the identified fault and compatible parts. Replacement is a separate option when the equipment condition or required work makes a repair unsuitable. We discuss the property requirements and proposed replacement connections before quoting; a larger system or different equipment is not assumed to fit the existing installation.

For an approved repair or replacement, the agreed scope includes the relevant connection work and checks for that work. We explain what has been completed and any remaining issues identified during the visit. If the visible leak appears to come from another pipe or fixture, our water leak detection service is the appropriate related starting point.

Our Perth plumbing and electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed trades. Licence details are available on request.

Hot Water Repairs & System Replacement Perth FAQs

No hot water: assess the system and the affected outlets

Loss of hot water at one outlet may need a different assessment from a whole-property failure. Plumbing connections, an electrical supply problem and equipment faults are distinct possibilities. Describe the system type if safely known. Gas, heat-pump, refrigerant or manufacturer-specific work requires the relevant scope and capability to be confirmed; a hot-water enquiry is not approval for every system type.

Can I buy a new hot-water unit before the assessment?

You can ask about a proposed product, but do not assume it suits the existing supply, connections or installation location. Confirm equipment compatibility, required trades and the full installation scope before purchase or approval.

Are gas and heat-pump systems automatically included?

No. Give the system type and proposed task. Any gas, refrigeration or other specialist component must be checked against the appropriate service capability and authorisation before work is accepted.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Is a hot-water problem plumbing or electrical?

It can be either, depending on the system and symptom. Describe the issue rather than trying to diagnose it yourself.

What should I send with a hot-water enquiry?

Include the property address, unit location, what changed, affected fixtures and any safely readable unit information.

Can I remove the cover to check the unit?

No. Do not remove covers or handle wiring.

Can you assess repair and replacement options at the same visit?

We assess the existing system and explain the repair scope. If replacement needs to be considered, we discuss equipment and connection requirements as a separate option in the written quote. No replacement is assumed from the initial enquiry.
