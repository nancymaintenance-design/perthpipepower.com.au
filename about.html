How a repair request moves forward

Clear details before work is arranged.

1. Tell us what you have noticed

Share the property suburb or address, the room or area affected, what you can see, hear or smell, and when the issue began. Photos can be helpful when they can be taken safely.

2. Include access and approval information

For managed properties, include the tenant or on-site contact, access notes, work-order reference and the person responsible for approvals. This helps us understand the practical context of the request.

3. Confirm scope and written quote

Our Perth team arranges an on-site assessment, identifies the fault or installation requirements and confirms the scope and written quote. We arrange the appropriately qualified trades for specialist or regulated work and confirm the responsible contractor and documentation before booking.
