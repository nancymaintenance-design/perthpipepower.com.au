Our point of view

Your home deserves proper attention.

For most of us, a home is much more than a place to live. It is where children grow up, families spend time together and people plan for the future. It is also one of life's most significant investments.

Keeping a property in good order can be difficult when life is busy. Small issues are easy to postpone; maintenance requests can be unclear; and a repair can involve an occupant, a property manager, access arrangements and an approval process. Ellis Services Group exists to make that first step more straightforward.

We ask customers to describe what they can see, hear or notice. They do not need to diagnose the cause before getting in touch.

Established in Perth

Built around integrity and fair pricing.

Established in 2011, Ellis Services Group was founded on a simple principle: maintenance work should be handled with integrity, clear communication and fair pricing. The home repair and renovation sector has not always earned the confidence it should. We want to raise that standard through practical service and follow-through.

Our aim is not to overcomplicate a property problem. It is to understand the issue, explain the relevant next step and help customers keep important details together from the first enquiry.

What guides our work

Integrity first. Clear information and respectful service.

Fair pricing. A practical approach to repair and maintenance enquiries.

Care for property. Treating a home or managed property as something worth looking after.

Frequently asked questions

When was Ellis Services Group established?

Ellis Services Group was established in 2011 and supports Perth homeowners, property managers and real-estate agencies with repair and maintenance enquiries.

What types of property enquiries can I send?

You can contact us about plumbing, electrical, home repair and property-maintenance matters. Describe the issue, property location and any access or approval details that are relevant.

Do I need to know the cause of the problem before I contact you?

No. Describe what you can observe, such as where the issue appears, when it started and whether there are any immediate safety concerns.

Can property managers and real-estate agencies contact Ellis Services Group?

Yes. Include the work-order reference, property address, occupant description, access contact and approval pathway where relevant.
