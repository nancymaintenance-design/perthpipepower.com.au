Choose the right repair starting point

A leaking fitting and a tripping circuit need different assessments. Use the plumbing or electrical overview for the work itself; use the area directory to arrange the visit. If both trades may be involved, describe both symptoms in one request. The proposed work and qualified trade arrangements still need to be confirmed.

Can I arrange plumbing and electrical work through one enquiry?

Yes. Tell us which rooms, fittings or equipment are affected and identify any immediate danger. We confirm the appropriate trade arrangements and quote the agreed tasks; a combined enquiry does not mean every task can be completed in one visit.

Do I need to diagnose the fault before calling?

No. Start with the Perth property location and what has changed. Photos are optional, and you should not enter a hazard or dismantle equipment to gather details.

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard or dismantle equipment to take them.
