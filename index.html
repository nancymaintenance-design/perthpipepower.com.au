A better first message

Include the details that help a request move forward.

  • Property address or Perth suburb
  • What you can see, hear or smell
  • When the issue began and whether it is worsening
  • On-site contact and access instructions
  • Photos, only if they can be taken safely

Put safety before diagnosis

If there is fire, smoke, active arcing or immediate danger, move to safety and call 000. Do not touch wet electrical equipment or exposed wiring.
