Signs to report when you contact us

Start with what is happening.

  • A tap drips after being turned off
  • Water appears around a handle, base or connection
  • A mixer is stiff, loose or no longer controls flow normally
  • One fixture has reduced flow or pressure
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

A tap or mixer problem is usually straightforward to describe: identify the room, fixture type, visible leak and whether the issue is constant or only appears while the fixture is being used. A photo can help if it can be taken without getting close to water or damaged components.

Ellis Services Group repairs and replaces taps and mixers in Perth kitchens, bathrooms, laundries, rental properties and commercial premises. We inspect the fixture on site and confirm the repair or replacement scope and written quote. If the fixture is leaking heavily or water is spreading, include that in the first message.

Site-specific scope

What affects the work and written quote?

The fitting type, condition, accessible connections and availability of compatible parts affect whether repair or replacement is practical. A surface fitting may be straightforward to access, while concealed connections require a different scope. We confirm the fitting, connection work and any access work in the quote.

Professional context

Clear observations support the right next step.

Drips, reduced flow, temperature-control changes, movement at a fitting and any water escaping into cabinetry or walls are useful starting observations.

A symptom can have more than one cause. We arrange an on-site assessment, check the reported symptoms and confirm the repair plan and written quote.

Safety and responsible work

If there is immediate danger, Call 000. Do not put yourself at risk to take a photo or investigate the cause.

We confirm the responsible WA-qualified contracting arrangement and current documentation, and arrange appropriately qualified trades before regulated work is booked.

Taps & mixers questions

Good to know before you enquire.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Frequently asked questions

Tap & Mixer Repairs Perth FAQs

Can you help with a dripping tap?

Yes. Tell us the fixture location, whether it drips constantly and any other changes you have noticed.

Should I try to dismantle a mixer?

No. It is safer to describe the problem and leave the fitting intact for assessment.

Do you work on rental-property fixtures?

Yes. Property managers can include work-order and access details.
