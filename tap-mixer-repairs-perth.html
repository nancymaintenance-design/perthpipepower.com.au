Kitchen, bathroom and laundry tap repairs

Dripping taps and leaking mixer fittings

A persistent drip and water appearing around the base or under a cabinet are different symptoms. We inspect the relevant fitting and accessible connections to define the repair. Tell us where the water appears and whether it occurs while the tap is running or when it is off; there is no need to dismantle the tap first.

Stiff controls, uneven delivery and fixture faults

Controls that become difficult to move, inconsistent flow and a fitting that no longer operates properly can involve different components or connection conditions. Our plumber assesses the fitting type and condition before selecting a repair. A symptom at one tap is not automatically evidence that the whole property needs new pipework.

Replacing a tap or mixer during an upgrade

If you want a replacement rather than a repair, we assess the mounting arrangement, accessible connections and compatibility of the proposed fitting. Send the product details if you already have a selection. For broader fixture and appliance connections, see our fixture and appliance installation services.

How we plan tap repairs and mixer replacement

We establish the fitting type, reported problem and available access above and below the fixture. Compatible parts and the condition of the existing fitting influence whether a repair is practical. We explain that assessment rather than assuming that either a small part or a complete replacement is always the right choice.

Match the fitting to the existing installation

Replacement needs to suit the fixture and connection arrangement, not just the appearance of the tap. Concealed connections or restricted cabinet access can change the work required. The quote identifies the fitting, connection work and any additional access so you know whether product supply and installation are both included.

After the authorised work, we check the repaired or replaced fitting and the relevant connections. We explain the completed scope and any separate issue observed around the installation. If the water source is unclear or extends beyond the fitting, our plumbing leak detection service addresses that investigation without treating it as a routine tap replacement.

Tap & Mixer Repairs Perth FAQs

A dripping tap, a base leak and a low-flow outlet

Describe where water appears: the outlet, handle, tap base or the connections below the fixture. A flow change at one tap is different from low pressure across the property. The fitting condition and compatible parts determine whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

  • New fixture and connection work
Does a dripping mixer always need replacing?

No. The cause, fitting condition and availability of compatible parts need assessment. Ask the quote to distinguish a repair from product supply and installation if replacement is recommended.

Can I supply the replacement mixer myself?

Discuss the product and existing connections first. Dimensions, mounting and compatibility can affect installation. The quote should identify who supplies the fitting and whether additional connection work is required.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Can you help with a dripping tap?

Yes. Tell us the fixture location, whether it drips constantly and any other changes you have noticed.

Should I try to dismantle a mixer?

No. It is safer to describe the problem and leave the fitting intact for assessment.

Do you work on rental-property fixtures?

Yes. Property managers can include work-order and access details.

Can you install a tap I have already purchased?

We assess the product specifications, mounting arrangement and connection compatibility before agreeing the installation. Tell us whether product supply or installation only is required so the written quote covers the right scope.
