Plumbing and electrical repairs in Northern Suburbs

For a Northern Suburbs repair request, state the suburb, property type and the part of the property affected. Add safe observations such as an affected room, fixture or circuit rather than an attempted diagnosis.

Plumbing repairs, drainage and hot water

Northern Suburbs plumbing bookings can combine day-to-day fixture repairs with a clear assessment of drainage and hot-water problems. A kitchen sink that drains slowly, a running cistern and a hot-water unit that loses temperature are separate tasks. We inspect the reported items and explain a scope for each, rather than assuming one repair will resolve every symptom.

For a blockage, the number of affected fixtures and whether it returns after ordinary use help define the investigation. Our blocked drain clearing service considers the affected drainage and any further repair identified during assessment. A leaking or continuously running toilet can instead require toilet and cistern repairs; a drainage blockage and a faulty flushing component are not interchangeable jobs.

For homes or rental properties with unreliable hot water, we assess the existing equipment and connections before recommending repair or replacement. Send the unit location and whether one or all hot outlets are affected when requesting hot-water repairs. Dripping mixers and leaking fixture connections can be included as a separate tap and mixer repair item.

Electrical repairs and installation

Recurring trips, partial power loss and equipment that stops operating call for diagnosis, not repeated resetting. Our safety switch and RCD repair service investigates the reported condition and separates an appliance-related issue from work on the fixed installation. Do not open the switchboard or keep forcing a device back on.

For planned home improvements, discuss the intended use of additional outlets or lighting, the rooms involved and whether you are replacing existing fittings or adding locations. Our lighting and power point installation service assesses the connection and wiring requirements before quoting. A list of proposed improvements can be assessed together, with repair and installation tasks priced in their agreed scope.

Work is carried out by appropriately licensed plumbing and electrical trades. Licence details are available on request. Our Perth team has an independent office and repair team within Ellis Services Group.

Written quote before work proceeds

We distinguish a repair visit from a planned installation when preparing the written quote. Existing equipment condition, compatible replacement parts, new cabling or pipe connections and access requirements affect the scope. A request for several items is recorded as separate tasks so you can decide which work to approve.

The final written quote confirms the included materials and work, along with any additional investigation or installation requirements. Changes identified on site are explained before extra work proceeds. On completion, we discuss what was repaired or installed and any separate items still requiring attention.

What to include in a repair request

For a rental maintenance booking, give the property address, occupant contact and the manager or owner who can authorise work. Record each item separately and identify the priority issue, such as loss of hot water or an active leak. We coordinate access and the approved scope with the nominated contact.

For a family home, note which rooms or fixtures must be accessible and any gate, pet or parking arrangements. Describe the fault from normal use; our team performs the assessment. Safe photos are useful but optional and must not involve removing fittings or approaching a hazard.
