Plumber and electrician in Northern Suburbs

Plumbing and electrical repairs in Northern Suburbs

For a Northern Suburbs repair request, state the suburb, property type and the part of the property affected. Add safe observations such as an affected room, fixture or circuit rather than an attempted diagnosis.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For a blocked drain, toilet or slow fixture, say whether the issue is limited to one point or affects several areas. Note any backing up, gurgling, odour or recurring slow drainage so the repair scope can be considered clearly.

For a hot-water concern, describe whether the change is temperature, pressure, supply or visible water around the system. Do not open the unit or adjust internal controls to investigate.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For a power fault or safety-switch concern, record what was affected when the change happened, if it can be observed safely. A switch that trips or will not reset, a circuit outage or a changed appliance connection are useful details for the repair request.

Do not repeatedly force a safety switch to reset, dismantle an outlet or touch damaged wiring. Keep clear and call 000 where there is immediate danger.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

For rentals and managed properties, include the tenant or on-site contact, access details and the person authorised to discuss the repair. This gives the Perth team the information needed to progress a written quote or booking.

If there is more than one issue, identify the priority concern first and keep plumbing and electrical symptoms in separate sentences.

Clear scope before work

Written quote before work proceeds

The written quote is based on the repair category, reported symptom, property access, equipment involved and any materials or additional work identified during assessment.

The scope and price are confirmed before work proceeds. Emergency, after-hours or unforeseen work is not assumed from a general website guide.
