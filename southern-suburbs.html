Plumber and electrician in Southern Suburbs

Plumbing and electrical repairs in Southern Suburbs

For a Southern Suburbs property, including Thornlie, report visible water, drainage changes and electrical faults as separate observations. Include the suburb, access contact and whether the request is for a home, rental, strata or managed property.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For a leak concern, describe the visible location, whether water is active and which room or fixture is affected. Avoid opening walls, fittings or equipment to locate the source.

For drainage problems, identify the affected fixture and whether water is slow, backing up or overflowing. These details help distinguish a single-fixture issue from a broader drainage concern.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For lighting, outlets or power faults, explain what has stopped working and whether the issue appears limited to one area. State any safe observations, such as a switch that will not stay on or a fitting that behaves differently.

Do not remove electrical covers, test wiring or approach a hazard. If there is smoke, fire, active arcing or immediate danger, move to safety and call 000.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

For a rental or managed property, the repair request is clearer when it includes the occupant contact, access notes, work-order reference and approval contact. This helps coordinate the property without asking an occupant to diagnose the issue.

Where a plumbing and electrical concern are reported at the same time, keep each symptom and affected area separate so the repair scope can be assessed accurately.
