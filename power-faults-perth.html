What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • Power has stopped in one room or part of the property
  • A circuit or safety switch trips repeatedly
  • An outlet, appliance or fixed item behaves differently
  • There is a burning smell, buzzing, heat, smoke or visible damage
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

Electrical faults should be reported from a safe position. Note what stopped working, the rooms affected, what was in use before the change and whether a safety switch operated. Do not repeatedly reset a device that trips again, open a switchboard or remove covers.

Ellis Services provides electrical repair and maintenance enquiries for Perth homes, rentals, strata and commercial properties. If there is smoke, fire, active arcing, exposed conductors or immediate danger, move away and call 000.

Frequently asked questions

Power Faults Perth FAQs

What information helps with a power-fault enquiry?

List the affected rooms or circuits, what happened first, what was in use and any warning signs you can observe safely.

Should I keep resetting a tripping circuit?

No. Avoid repeatedly resetting an electrical device that trips again.

What if I smell burning or see smoke?

Move to safety and call 000 if there is immediate danger.
