Plumber and electrician in Western Suburbs

Plumbing and electrical repairs in Western Suburbs

For a Western Suburbs repair request, distinguish fixture concerns such as taps, toilets and drainage from lighting, switches and power points. Include the affected room or outdoor area and an access contact.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For taps, mixers, toilets and showers, describe the fitting and the visible symptom: dripping, running, leaking, loose, slow draining or not operating as expected. Include whether water is active or contained safely.

For blocked drains, note whether the issue affects one fixture or several, whether water is backing up and whether there is an odour. Do not use chemicals as a substitute for a repair assessment.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For lighting or power-point concerns, identify the affected fitting or outlet and what changed. A light that has stopped working, an outlet that feels loose or a circuit that trips are useful observations when reported from a safe position.

Never remove a fitting, touch damaged wiring or attempt a repair. For smoke, active arcing or immediate danger, move clear and call 000.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

For managed homes, rentals and strata properties, provide access notes, parking or gate instructions where relevant, plus the tenant or on-site contact and approval contact. This supports a practical repair booking.

List each repair item, such as a tap repair, blocked drain or power-point issue. Our Perth team checks the reported items and coordinates the service arrangements in the agreed repair scope.
