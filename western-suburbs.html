Plumbing and electrical repairs in Western Suburbs

For a Western Suburbs repair request, distinguish fixture concerns such as taps, toilets and drainage from lighting, switches and power points. Include the affected room or outdoor area and an access contact.

Plumbing repairs, drainage and hot water

Western Suburbs plumbing service covers repairs and replacement connections for bathrooms, kitchens and laundries. For a dripping mixer, a loose fitting or a leaking shower connection, our tap and mixer repair service assesses the fitting, accessible connections and suitable repair or replacement options. Product selection and installation are discussed separately where required.

A running cistern, slow refill or ineffective flush calls for toilet and cistern repairs, while water backing up through drainage may require blocked drain clearing. We explain the distinction during assessment so the proposed work addresses the fault rather than replacing an unrelated fixture.

For a kitchen or laundry improvement, our fixture and appliance connection service considers the existing water, waste and relevant electrical connections. Cabinet access, changed fixture positions and alterations to pipework affect the scope. We do not assume that a connection quote includes a full room renovation or restoration of finishes.

Electrical repairs and installation

For lighting changes and new outlets, our lighting and power point repairs and installation service assesses the planned location, existing fitting and connection requirements. Replacement and additional installation are separate tasks; adding a fitting can require wiring or circuit work beyond swapping an existing component.

We assess reported faults as well as planned upgrades. Flickering lighting, an outlet fault and loss of power need investigation before choosing a replacement product. For connected controls or automation, discuss the devices and intended functions through our smart home electrical service. Equipment compatibility and connection scope are confirmed rather than assumed.

Work is carried out by appropriately licensed plumbing and electrical trades. Licence details are available on request. Our Perth team has an independent office and repair team within Ellis Services Group.

Written quote before work proceeds

The quote distinguishes repair, replacement product supply and installation. Changes to connections, additional pipe or cable routes, access and agreed making-good work are recorded separately. This helps you compare the same scope when deciding between repair and replacement options.

We explain the proposed work before approval and discuss any additional tasks identified during the visit before they proceed. Published ranges are indicative planning information; the written quote confirms the final scope and price. On completion, we explain the work carried out and any separate outstanding items.

What to include in a repair request

For an owner-occupied property, provide a room-by-room list of repairs or proposed changes. Separate urgent faults from improvements you want quoted. Clear priorities help define the visit and prevent a small fixture repair from becoming an unspecified renovation request.

For rentals or strata-managed homes, include the occupant contact, access information and authorised decision-maker. Photos of existing fittings can be useful when safely taken, but our team confirms the actual installation requirements on site. Do not dismantle fittings to photograph the connections.
