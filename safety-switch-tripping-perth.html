Safety-switch fault finding and RCD repairs

Repeated tripping or a switch that will not stay on

Repeated operation of a protective device is a symptom that needs investigation. Tell us which areas have lost power and whether the problem began during normal use or after a change at the property. We assess the relevant circuits and connections without asking you to force the switch on or continue recreating the problem.

Connected equipment and wiring concerns

The assessment distinguishes a concern involving connected equipment from one involving the fixed installation or protective device. A tripping switch alone does not establish which item is responsible. We explain the investigation findings so the repair targets the identified issue rather than treating a new switch as a universal fix.

Safety Switch & RCD Repairs Perth FAQs

Repeated trips do not identify the failed component

An RCD or safety switch and a circuit breaker perform different protective roles. Note the label only if it is safely readable; do not open covers to identify the device. Appliances, wiring and the protective device can require different investigation before replacement is considered.

  • Electrical fault investigation
  • What to record when a safety switch trips
Is a safety switch the same as a circuit breaker?

No. The devices have different protective functions. If you are unsure which has operated, report the affected rooms and any safely visible label. The electrician identifies the device and assessment needed.

Can replacing the RCD guarantee that tripping will stop?

No. Tripping can be associated with another part of the installation or connected equipment. The cause needs assessment; replacing the protective device without that assessment may not resolve the fault.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Why does a safety switch trip?

There can be several causes. The most useful first step is to record what was happening and avoid trying to diagnose fixed wiring yourself.

Can I open the switchboard?

No. Do not open switchboards or remove covers.

What should I tell Ellis Services Group?

Include the affected areas, when it happened, what was in use and any safely observable warning signs.

Will replacing the safety switch stop the tripping?

Not necessarily. Tripping can relate to more than the device itself. We investigate the relevant fault and explain whether device replacement or another identified repair is appropriate before quoting.
