Safety Switch Keeps Tripping in Perth

A safe, plain-English starting point when a safety switch will not stay on or trips again after being reset.

Call 0413 477 667
What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • The safety switch moves off and will not stay on
  • It trips after a particular appliance or room is used
  • More than one part of the property has lost power
  • Water, dampness or a recent event may be connected to the change
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

A safety switch that continues to trip is a reason to stop and record what happened. Note which rooms are affected, what was being used before it operated and whether there has been rain, cleaning, a leak or water ingress nearby. Do not remove the switchboard cover or test fixed wiring.

Safety Switch Keeps Tripping in Perth FAQs

Why does a safety switch trip?

There can be several causes. The most useful first step is to record what was happening and avoid trying to diagnose fixed wiring yourself.

Can I open the switchboard?

No. Do not open switchboards or remove covers.

What should I tell Ellis Services Group?

Include the affected areas, when it happened, what was in use and any safely observable warning signs.
