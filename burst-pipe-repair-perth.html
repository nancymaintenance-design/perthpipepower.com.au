What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • Water is actively escaping from a pipe or connection
  • Water is spreading across a floor, ceiling or wall
  • A pipe or fitting has visibly failed
  • Water has stopped or pressure has changed suddenly
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

A burst pipe can affect more than the point where it becomes visible. If it is safe and you already know how to isolate the water supply at the property, you may do so. Do not take risks to find a valve, remove access panels or enter a wet area near electrical equipment.

When you contact Ellis Services Group, state whether water is active, the part of the property affected, what you can see and whether there is safe access. Property managers can also include the work order, occupant contact and approval details so the request stays together.

Frequently asked questions

Burst Pipe Repair Perth FAQs

What should I do first if a pipe bursts?

Put people and electrical safety first. If it is safe and familiar, isolate the water supply; otherwise keep clear and contact us with the details.

When should I use the water leak page instead?

Use Water Leak Detection for an unexplained leak or damp area where there is no visible failed pipe. Use this page when water is actively escaping or a pipe or fitting has visibly failed.

Do I need to know the pipe type?

No. A description of the location, water flow and affected area is more useful than a guess about the cause.

Can a property manager make the booking?

Yes. Include access, occupant and work-order details in the enquiry.
