Why can an online guide price differ from the written quote?

A guide range describes a standard scope; the property may need different access, materials, investigation or additional work. Ask the team to identify the inclusions and approval boundaries in the written quote rather than treating an online range as a fixed offer.

Can I approve investigation without approving every possible repair?

Discuss the initial assessment scope and the person authorised to approve it. If further repair or replacement is proposed, ask what needs separate agreement. An enquiry or access arrangement alone is not approval of unlimited work.

What is Ellis Services Group?

Ellis Services Group is part of the Ellis Group, with a dedicated Perth repair team providing plumbing and electrical repairs and maintenance across Perth for homes, rentals, strata properties and commercial premises.

Which areas does Ellis Services Group cover?

Our dedicated Perth repair team provides plumbing and electrical services across Perth. Include the property suburb and service required so we can prepare for the work.

What information should I provide?

Provide the property address, affected area, factual description, when it began, whether it is active or worsening, and the best access contact. Property managers should include a work-order reference and approval pathway.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach exposed wiring, active sparking, wet electrical equipment or another hazard to take a photo.

What if I do not know whether the problem is plumbing or electrical?

Describe what you observe rather than guessing. Our Perth team will identify the appropriate plumbing or electrical service path.

Should I use the blocked drain or blocked toilet page?

Use Blocked Drains & Toilets for slow drainage, gurgling, backups or a blocked toilet. Use Toilet Repairs when the concern is specifically a running, leaking, slow-filling or non-functioning toilet.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Put people and electrical safety first. If it is safe and familiar, isolate the water supply; otherwise keep clear and use the Burst Pipe Repair page to share the affected area and active water details.

What should I do if a safety switch keeps tripping?

Do not repeatedly reset it. Note which areas lost power and what equipment was being used. Do not open the switchboard or work on fixed wiring.

When is Ellis Services Group available?

Ellis Services Group is available for Perth plumbing and electrical enquiries every day from 00:00 to 23:59.

Are licence details available?

Services are provided by appropriately licensed plumbing and electrical tradespeople. Current licence details are available on request for procurement or property management.

When should I call 000?

Call 000 for life-threatening emergencies, fire, smoke, active arcing or immediate danger. Move to a safe location first. Once the immediate hazard is made safe, contact Ellis Services Group to arrange the repair assessment.
