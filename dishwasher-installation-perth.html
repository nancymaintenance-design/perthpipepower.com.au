Common situations and what to report

Replacing a dishwasher in the same space

An old dishwasher is being replaced and the connection under the kitchen sink is leaking. The new appliance and the existing tap, hose connection and drain arrangement need to be considered together. A same-space replacement does not guarantee that every existing connection can be reused.

A new location or water backing into the sink

A first dishwasher installation may need new supply or drain work and suitable power. If both sink bowls fill when the dishwasher drains, report the drainage symptom as well as the installation request; replacing the machine alone may not address a downstream blockage.

These are illustrative situations, not customer case studies or diagnoses. Report what you have noticed; you do not need to identify the cause before contacting us.

Assessment, work plan and written quote

  1. Describe the job. Give the suburb, affected equipment or planned installation, when it began and whether the condition is worsening. Add safe access and the person who can approve the work.
  2. Assess the installation. We check the relevant equipment and connections on site. Concealed work, product compatibility and the cause of a fault cannot be guaranteed from an enquiry or photograph.
  3. Agree the scope. The written quote identifies attendance or investigation, the selected equipment, materials and agreed work. Confirm GST, disposal, access, testing and any making good that applies to the specific job.
  4. Approve changes separately. If findings change the required work, we explain the additional scope and charge before proceeding. Confirm the checks and completion information needed for a rental or managed property.

Published standard-hours guide ranges elsewhere on this site are not a fixed price for this unseen job. Emergency repair charges follow the quoted plan. There is no assumed free attendance, unlimited investigation or universal hourly rate.

Can you supply the dishwasher and take away the old one?

Yes. Supply, removal and taking away the old unit are available within the agreed installation package. Confirm the selected appliance, access and the included connection work in the written quote.

Is a leaking dishwasher hose a plumbing issue or an appliance repair issue?

A leak at the supply valve, connection or external hose may involve plumbing; a leak inside the machine may require appliance repair. The source needs assessment. A wet cupboard alone does not establish which trade or component is responsible.

Can you check the water and drain connections before installation?

Yes. We assess the existing connections and proposed product before agreeing the installation. Report leaking valves, slow drainage or previous overflows so those issues are included in the assessment.

Does dishwasher installation include cabinet changes or a new power point?

Not automatically. New pipework, a new power point, circuit changes and cabinet alterations must be identified and included or excluded in the quote. The supplied dishwasher alone does not define that additional work.
