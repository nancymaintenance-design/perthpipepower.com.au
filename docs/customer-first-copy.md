# Customer-first bilingual copy maintenance

Scope: Perth Pipe Power, Ellis Services Group. Review English and Chinese together.

- Start with the customer's problem, our actual service and the next enquiry step. Use We / 我们 for our service and process. Keep brand names in identity fields and retain the speaker in customer quotations.
- Check business facts before rewriting a claim. Current office, service areas, hours, supply/removal arrangements, licences, prices and fees each need their own evidence. An old webpage is a claim to check, not proof of a new capability.
- Explain what the written quote covers: attendance or investigation, the repair, product supply, installation and any separate access or restoration. Keep the AUD amount, charging unit, GST and real conditions together. Do not add free attendance, fixed totals, response times or certificates without confirmation.
- Treat a submitted enquiry as an enquiry. Confirm appointment and work separately. Preserve the real phone, email, form contract and recipient.
- Use natural Australian English. In Chinese, distinguish 上门 from website access, 进场 from electrical connections, 出水点 from 排水口, and a running cistern from a machine operating. Read the complete sentence after terminology changes.
- A how-question needs an explanation, not an answer beginning with an unrelated No. Describe our assessment and the approval step while retaining actual limitations.
- Keep safety advice short and actionable. Do not remove negations or conditions because a search flags them. Keep privacy and legal text separate from ordinary copy editing.
- Edit canonical page content and the source translation dictionary. Check visible copy, metadata, FAQ JSON-LD and article abstracts, plus existing feeds. Repackage locale hashes only after reviewing the paired text. Local packaging is not user approval to release.
- Check the full rendered page, including mobile navigation, sticky enquiry form, FAQ expansion, language switch and final footer. Do not submit real enquiries to test copy.
- Keep route, canonical, hreflang, robots, sitemap, JSON-LD identity, service hierarchy and the removed-link policy intact unless a separately scoped change is authorised.
- Use exact approved text-unit changes for audit evidence. Phrase searches find candidates; they are never a global deletion or brand-replacement rule.

Local validation: build, typecheck, SEO validation, relevant route/form/localisation tests, the bilingual-abstract regression, and the report's output-scope and browser checks. Historical release snapshots protect earlier releases; before a future release, review how this exact candidate is integrated into the full regression baseline rather than overwriting snapshots wholesale.
