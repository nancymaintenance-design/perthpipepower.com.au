Plumber and electrician in Eastern Suburbs

Plumbing and electrical repairs in Eastern Suburbs

For an Eastern Suburbs electrical repair request, identify the affected area or circuit only if it can be observed safely. Pair that with the property access contact and any separate plumbing concern.

Plumbing repairs, blocked drains, leak concerns and hot-water problems

For a hot-water, tap, toilet or drain concern, describe the affected fixture and the change you can see or hear. Examples include reduced hot water, a running toilet, a dripping tap, slow drainage or visible water around a system.

Do not remove panels, disconnect equipment or open fittings to investigate. A factual description of the symptom, location and timing is more useful than a guessed cause.

Electrical repair information

Electrical repairs, fault finding, safety switches and power points

For a switchboard or safety-switch concern, report which part of the property is affected and whether the switch will reset, if known. Do not repeatedly reset a switch or remove a cover to inspect the board.

For lighting and power points, identify the fitting or outlet and whether the issue appears limited to that point or affects more of the property. Keep clear of damaged wiring or equipment affected by water.

Homes, rentals and managed properties

What to include in a repair request

For homes, rentals and managed properties, include the property address, best access contact and the person authorised to discuss the repair. This helps the Perth team move from a symptom report to a defined scope.

If there are both electrical and plumbing concerns, list them separately so safety-critical electrical work is not obscured by a general maintenance description.

Clear scope before work

Written quote before work proceeds

The repair scope is confirmed from the reported issue, safe observations, property access and any assessment required. A written quote sets out the work and price before work proceeds.

Additional work is only added after it has been explained. For an immediate electrical danger, move clear and call 000 rather than trying to gather more detail.

Eastern Suburbs plumber and electrician FAQ

What helps with an Eastern Suburbs safety-switch repair request?

Include the property address, what changed, affected areas and any safe observations about the switch.

Should a switchboard concern be listed with other repairs?

Yes, but identify the switchboard concern separately from plumbing or general maintenance items.
