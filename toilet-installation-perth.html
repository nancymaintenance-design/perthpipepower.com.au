Common situations and what to report

Replacing a toilet rather than a cistern part

A continuously refilling cistern may be repairable without replacing the whole toilet. Tell us why you want replacement, including damage, leaks or a planned change. We compare the repair option with the proposed installation instead of treating every running toilet as a replacement job.

Checking the proposed suite before ordering

A new toilet has to suit the existing waste connection, water connection and available space. Photos taken safely may help preparation, but measurements and connection requirements still need assessment. Product selection alone does not establish that it will fit the property.

These are illustrative situations, not customer case studies or diagnoses. Report what you have noticed; you do not need to identify the cause before contacting us.

Assessment, work plan and written quote

  1. Describe the job. Give the suburb, affected equipment or planned installation, when it began and whether the condition is worsening. Add safe access and the person who can approve the work.
  2. Assess the installation. We check the relevant equipment and connections on site. Concealed work, product compatibility and the cause of a fault cannot be guaranteed from an enquiry or photograph.
  3. Agree the scope. The written quote identifies attendance or investigation, the selected equipment, materials and agreed work. Confirm GST, disposal, access, testing and any making good that applies to the specific job.
  4. Approve changes separately. If findings change the required work, we explain the additional scope and charge before proceeding. Confirm the checks and completion information needed for a rental or managed property.

Published standard-hours guide ranges elsewhere on this site are not a fixed price for this unseen job. Emergency repair charges follow the quoted plan. There is no assumed free attendance, unlimited investigation or universal hourly rate.

Do you supply the toilet and remove the old one?

Yes. The agreed installation package includes the selected toilet, removal and taking away the old unit. The written quote identifies the product and any additional connection or access work.

Can a running toilet be repaired without replacing the whole toilet?

Often the fault needs assessment of cistern components rather than an immediate new suite. We explain the findings and available repair or replacement scope before you approve the work.

Does a blocked toilet mean I need a new toilet?

No. A blockage, a cistern fault and a damaged toilet suite are different problems. Drainage needs assessment where water backs up or other outlets are affected; replacing a suite does not automatically clear a downstream blockage.

Can I choose the toilet myself?

Yes, but confirm compatibility before ordering. Waste and water connection positions, dimensions and access can affect whether the selected model suits the existing installation.
