Smoke-alarm maintenance and replacement services

Chirping and reported fault indications

Tell us the alarm location, the pattern of the sound and any safely readable information. An intermittent chirp and an active alarm are not the same situation. We assess the equipment and reported indication rather than treating every sound as a request for the same replacement part. If there is smoke or immediate danger, act on the emergency rather than waiting for maintenance.

Damaged units and planned replacement

The alarm type, accessible age information and existing installation influence replacement requirements. We review the equipment and explain the proposed unit and connection work before quoting. Do not detach a hardwired alarm or open wiring to identify it; visible information and the room location are enough to begin the booking request.

Rental and managed-property maintenance

Property managers can send a work order with the affected locations, occupant contact and approval requirements. An individual alarm fault and a broader property assessment are different scopes. We clarify the work being requested instead of describing a single repair as proof that every alarm and every legal obligation at the property has been checked.

How we assess smoke-alarm work at your property

Our electrician reviews the reported fault, equipment type and access to the relevant installation. We explain whether the proposed work is maintenance, fault investigation or replacement, and identify the associated electrical work. The job scope matters: it determines which units and locations are included and what findings the visit can properly address.

Replacement equipment and installation requirements

Equipment selection and location requirements need to be considered for the property and proposed work. We discuss any connection changes or additional installation work before approval. DFES smoke-alarm information provides public safety guidance; it is not a substitute for assessing the installation or confirming a specific property scope.

After completing the authorised work, we explain the checks relevant to that work and any remaining issue identified. Where the enquiry also involves lost power or circuit concerns, our Perth electrical services cover the related assessment. For agency maintenance, the completed work should be read against the agreed work order, not as an unspecified whole-property certification.

Our Perth plumbing and electrical work is carried out by appropriately licensed trades. Licence details are available on request.

Smoke Alarm Repairs, Replacement & Maintenance Perth FAQs

A fault report is not the same as a whole-property compliance check

Tell us whether you are reporting a particular alarm fault, planning replacement or requesting a defined property-wide check. For rentals, include the owner or agent approval contact. Do not remove a hardwired alarm or assume that replacing one unit establishes compliance for the whole premises.

  • Rental-property access and approval
  • Related electrical maintenance
Can alarm and safety-switch work be discussed in the same request?

Yes. List the alarm locations and the separate safety-switch concern or requested check. Confirm the assessment, included tests and reporting requirements; one task does not automatically include the other.

Does replacing one smoke alarm certify the whole property?

No. A single-unit replacement and a defined whole-property assessment are different scopes. State the property context and any required documentation before agreeing the work.

Do I need to diagnose the problem first?

No. Describe the observable change and property context. We inspect the affected area on site to establish the cause and confirm the repair scope. If more access is needed, we agree it with you first.

Can I send photos?

Photos are optional: email safely taken photos to maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com. You can contact us without photos. Do not approach a hazard, remove covers or dismantle equipment to take a photo.

Can you help with a smoke alarm that keeps beeping?

Yes. Tell us where it is, what it is doing and when the behaviour began.

Should I remove a hardwired smoke alarm?

No. Do not remove a hardwired unit or handle its wiring.

Can a property manager send a maintenance request?

Yes. Include the property address, occupant access and approval details.

Does repairing one alarm assess the whole property?

No. The agreed scope identifies the units and work included. A broader property assessment or replacement plan is a separate request, which we discuss and define before quoting.
