What you may notice

Start with what is happening.

  • A smoke alarm beeps or shows a fault indication
  • An alarm has changed behaviour after a battery change or power event
  • A property manager needs maintenance information for a rental
  • A fixed alarm requires attention at a home or commercial property
Perth service information

Clear information makes the next step easier.

A smoke alarm is a safety device, so it is useful to describe the alarm location, the sound or indication, when it started and whether it is a single alarm or more than one. Do not remove a hardwired unit or work on its wiring.

Ellis Services Group can discuss smoke-alarm maintenance and repair enquiries in Perth. For property-managed requests, include the property address, access contact and work-order or approval details.

Smoke Alarm Maintenance Perth FAQs

Can you help with a smoke alarm that keeps beeping?

Yes. Tell us where it is, what it is doing and when the behaviour began.

Should I remove a hardwired smoke alarm?

No. Do not remove a hardwired unit or handle its wiring.

Can a property manager send a maintenance request?

Yes. Include the property address, occupant access and approval details.
