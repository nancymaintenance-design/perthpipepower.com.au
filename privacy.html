Privacy policy

How Ellis Services Group handles information you provide through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and property address or suburb, together with the service details you choose to provide.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, discuss plumbing or electrical work, arrange access where appropriate and keep records connected with the enquiry or completed work.

Keeping information secure

Provide only information that is relevant to the request. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information or take photos that place you near an electrical, water or other safety hazard.

Contact and updates

For a privacy question or to update information you have submitted, contact Ellis Services Group at maxinemaintenance.au@outlook.com or call 0413 477 667.
